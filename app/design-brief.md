# La Risata — design brief

- Product: standalone official website for La Risata slice pizza burger & Fries.
- Animation mode: non-animated (user explicitly selected simple website).
- Goal: convert local visitors into dine-in customers first, with clear secondary paths for takeaway, delivery, TheFork and WhatsApp once owner URLs are supplied.
- Audience: people in the local area looking for pizza, burgers and casual street food.
- Reference: Loft 128 restaurant page; borrow editorial confidence and full-bleed food storytelling, not its identity or layout verbatim.
- Brand: street food / industrial / old-school / handmade. User's established colors: dark brown, rust, warm orange, cream. Accent: muted rust red. One warm palette across page.
- Typography: bold condensed/grotesk display with readable sans body; restrained vintage serif only for selected food/editorial accents.
- Composition: bold poster-like hero, asymmetrical food illustration, large typography, distinct section layouts, strong dine-in CTA.
- Sections: hero, food categories, house-made / about, dine-in invitation, practical info/contact, footer.
- Motion: subtle CSS reveal/hover only, disabled under prefers-reduced-motion. No scroll-scrub or complex animation.
- Asset note: Higgsfield image generation was unavailable because the workspace had no credits. Use original authored vector artwork as a temporary bespoke visual; replace with real restaurant photos before final marketing launch.
- Missing owner-supplied data to finalize: phone/WhatsApp, TheFork booking URL, ordering/delivery URL, current opening hours, current menu/prices, real photos. Confirmed venue address: Via Ortona 8, Pescara, Italy.
